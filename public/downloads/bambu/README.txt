WONGO / BAMBU P1S / 20 mm

Open each Plates/*.3mf as a PROJECT in Bambu Studio, not as geometry only.
Confirm P1S, 0.4 mm nozzle, build plate and matching Flashforge PLA Matte spool.
Slice and preview before printing. Projects are editable and contain no G-code.
Print each colour once for 40 pieces (2-4 players); twice for 80 (5-8 players).

0.10 mm layers including first layer; 3 walls; 5% gyroid; 0.8 mm top/bottom.
No supports, brim or prime tower; by-layer printing; at least 6 mm object gaps. All objects form one compact centered group, inside the central 200 x 200 mm region.
Textured PEI starting profile: nozzle 220 C, bed 55 C, volumetric cap 8 mm3/s.
The filament settings derive from Generic PLA and are not physically calibrated.
Use your calibrated filament profile if available. Print a small sample first.
Each 20 mm voxel has a 0.5 mm 45-degree chamfer, with shallow visible seam grooves. Lattice pitch and shape IDs are unchanged. Surface inset 0.15 mm also opens concave slots; do not uniformly scale these models. Print a yellow Gate #04 fit sample before a full run. No embossed IDs. Previous slicer estimates describe the v1 geometry; re-slice these v2 plates for current time and filament.

Use ONLY matching 20 mm puzzle grids at 100% / actual size.
Cards: downloads/puzzles/wongo-d6-20mm.pdf or the companion HTML.
A4 landscape, two A5 cards per side. Duplex: flip on SHORT edge.
Easy puzzles on fronts, hard on backs; backs deliberately reverse the two slots.
Cut along the centre line after checking one test sheet. No solutions on backs.
D6 set: 288 pages = 144 A4 sheets = 288 double-sided A5 cards in 36 packs. Six choices share each footprint. Use one common die and one common difficulty; keep printed packs and four-player blocks together. The original single-puzzle catalogue remains available separately.

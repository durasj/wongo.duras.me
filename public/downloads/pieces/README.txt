WONGO PRINTABLE PIECES

16 distinct shapes. All STL files use millimetres; unit cube side 20 mm.
Each STL is an outer union surface: no internal coincident faces.
All are oriented with flat supported layers; supports are not required for ideal geometry.
Print quantities are in manifest.json: quantity4 (40 total) or quantity8 (80 total).
Use the 40-piece pool for 2-4 players or the 80-piece pool for 5-8 players.
These are a shared inventory, not 16 pieces per player.

FILAMENT: Flashforge PLA Matte, 1.75 mm
IDs 01-04: Mango Yellow (9 / 18 copies for 4 / 8 players)
IDs 05-08: Blue (072C) (11 / 22 copies)
IDs 09-12: Red (10 / 20 copies)
IDs 13-16: Mint Green (10 / 20 copies)
Screen hex colours in manifest.json are visual approximations, not calibrated filament values.
STL files contain geometry only; select the matching filament in your slicer.

Start with PLA, 0.1 mm layers, 3 walls, 5% gyroid infill; use your calibrated filament profile.
Print a small fit sample before committing to the full set. Inspect first-layer flare.
Lattice pitch and occupied cells are unchanged. Only yellow Gate #04 has 0.15 mm relief per inner slot wall, widening its nominal 20 mm opening to 20.3 mm. Its exterior dimensions and every other model are unchanged. Compatible with previously printed unscaled 20 mm pieces. Each voxel has a 0.5 mm 45-degree edge chamfer; joining faces are fused, leaving visible shallow seam grooves. The physical solid is intentionally smaller at edges than the exact solver volume.
SCAD files expose the unit size. Scale all pieces AND future puzzle cards together.
